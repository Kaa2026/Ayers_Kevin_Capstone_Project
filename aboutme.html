Kevin is an illustrator who started doodling in his notebooks during high school, rather than study for his tests. Since then, he has been drawing when not working or studying, and often takes an art class in the summer when not busy.

His favorite mediums to work with are usually traditional tools such as: graphite, charcoal, ink, and most recently, alcohol markers. Digital tools include his XP-Pen 21 inch drawing monitor and Clip Studio Paint.
When he’s not drawing he is tinkering in Unreal Engine 5 and watching cartoons.
